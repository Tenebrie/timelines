Basic navigation in Neverkin is handled through two primary components: the **navigator** and the **sidebar**.

## Navigator

The **navigator** is a floating panel at the top of your screen that provides you access to different features in the global scope.

<Image src={baseNavigator01} alt="The top bar, showing the main features and options available in Neverkin." />

Listing the controls from left to right:

- **Worlds hamburger dropdown** - a quick way to switch between worlds.
- **World button** - returns you to the last opened world.
- **Home button** - returns you to the home page. Clicking the arrow gives you more options.
- **Tools** - a selection of totally random tools that may or may not be useful.
- **Docs** (not pictured) - these very pages!
- **Admin** (only for admin users) - administration tools.

On the right side, you can see:
- **Dark mode toggle** - switches between light and dark mode.
- **Notification bell** - that's where you get occasional notifications.
- **User account dropdown** - clicking here allows you to access your profile and user settings.

## World sidebar

The **sidebar** is a panel visible while inside a world. Here you can switch between different views and main features of the app.

<Image src={worldNavigator01} alt="The world sidebar, showing the main in-world views." />

Listing your options top-to-bottom:

- **Wiki** - where you can do most of your writing
- **Mindmap** - a place to visualize your ideas (or make some cool charts)
- **Timeline** - a chronological view of your events
- **World settings** - a little view to handle world-related stuff, like changing the name or sending out invites.
